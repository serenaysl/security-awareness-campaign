# Security Awareness Campaign Email

Subject: Security Awareness Week - Think Before You Click

Hi everyone,

This week we are focusing on a few simple cybersecurity habits that can help prevent common security incidents.

During the week, we will cover:

- How to recognize phishing emails
- Better password practices
- Why MFA is important
- Common social engineering techniques
- What to do when something looks suspicious

The main idea of the campaign is simple:

Think before you click.

Before opening an unexpected link or attachment, take a few seconds to check the sender and the request.

Please also remember:

- Do not share passwords or verification codes
- Use unique passwords
- Enable MFA when available
- Verify unusual requests
- Report suspicious activity

At the end of the week, there will be a short security quiz covering the main topics.

Thanks for taking part.
